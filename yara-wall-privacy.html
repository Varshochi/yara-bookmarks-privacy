Varshochi YARA WALL

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Varshochi YARA WALL is a private, single-user automation used by the account owner to read the owner's Pinterest boards and Pins and sync selected wallpaper images into a private self-hosted workflow and Google Drive.

1. Data the app accesses

  • The authenticated Pinterest account's own board names, board IDs, Pin IDs and Pin metadata.
  • Image URLs and related media information for Pins saved by the authenticated account.
  • OAuth access tokens required to authenticate requests to the Pinterest API.

2. How the data is used

  • Detect newly saved Pins on selected wallpaper boards.
  • Retrieve the corresponding image assets for personal wallpaper use.
  • Prevent duplicate processing and keep basic synchronization history.
  • Store selected wallpaper images in the account owner's private Google Drive.

3. Read-only Pinterest use

The app is intended to use Pinterest data in a read-only manner. It does not create, edit, delete, publish, comment on, or otherwise modify Pinterest content.

4. Storage and processing

Automation runs in a private, self-hosted n8n environment controlled by the account owner. Selected image files may be stored in the account owner's private Google Drive. Limited synchronization metadata may be retained only as needed to identify previously processed Pins and prevent duplicates.

5. Third-party services

The app uses:

  • Pinterest API to read the authenticated account's own Pins and boards.
  • n8n for private workflow automation.
  • Google Drive for private storage of selected wallpaper images.

Data is not provided to unrelated third parties.

6. No sale, advertising or profiling

  • No sale of Pinterest data or personal information.
  • No personalized advertising.
  • No third-party marketing.
  • No analytics or behavioral profiling of other Pinterest users.

7. Data retention and deletion

Processed wallpaper images and synchronization metadata are retained only for the owner's personal wallpaper workflow. The owner can remove stored images and workflow records at any time. Pinterest access can also be revoked by disconnecting the app from the Pinterest account.

8. Security

Authentication credentials and access tokens are stored in the owner's private self-hosted automation environment and are not exposed publicly.

9. Users

Varshochi YARA WALL is not offered as a public service and is not designed for multiple external users. It is for single-user personal API access by the authenticated account owner.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, visit rshochi.com.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Varshochi YARA WALL Pinterest integration and its private self-hosted automation workflow.
